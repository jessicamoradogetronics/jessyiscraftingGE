Inspiration

The worlds that shape my work

I have always loved painting and making crafts since I was little, but what I do today has a common origin: Kingdom Come Deliverance

Kingdom Come: Deliverance

The game that laid the foundations for all of this

Kingdom Come: Deliverance is, probably, the reason JessyIsCrafting exists the way it does today. The power of a video game as art in itself is very powerful and inspiring.

I discovered the landscapes of Bohemia, so green and similar to my homeland that they made me feel at home. Its characters, its art direction and its soundtrack are the perfect complements to bring colour to the work.

An infinite world of inspiration, with which you can create practically anything.

But I needed more, and like me, hundreds of people did too. I discovered the world of fanfics, and I immediately knew it was for me. Not only reading, but writing my own stories and sharing them with people.

The same goes for art. I don't remember ever having such a creative explosion as the one I've had with Kingdom Come Deliverance. I'm not only talking about paintings: I dared to make my first digital drawings and explore channels I had never tried before.

Reading & writing

The other half of my brain

If I'm not painting, I'm probably reading, or writing. I've always loved filling in the gaps a story leaves untold: what a side character thinks, what happened between two scenes, what was felt in a moment the game barely touches.

And since I needed even more, I couldn't help it: I travelled to Bohemia! I needed to feel those virtual settings on my own skin, the ones I spent hours and hours in, enjoying every corner as if I were actually there.

I decided to visit every place in both games, and none of them disappointed me. The Bohemian meadow is spectacular and the mountainous landscape of Trosky is one of my favourites. I absolutely recommend making this trip once in a lifetime.
