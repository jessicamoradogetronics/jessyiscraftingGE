Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before ordering

If you have a question that isn't answered here, feel free to reach out through the contact form.

Which countries do you ship to? +

I ship as standard to all of Spain, the rest of the European Union and the United Kingdom. If you live anywhere else, message me before placing your order: we'll figure it out together, since customs fees and delivery times can vary quite a bit.

How long does it take to prepare my order? +

Pieces that are already finished usually leave my workshop within 2-4 business days. Custom orders, illustration commissions or made-to-order painted figures normally need between 1 and 3 weeks, depending on how complex the order is and my waiting list at that moment. Around Christmas, I'd recommend booking well in advance.

Can I return or exchange an order? +

Non-personalised catalogue pieces can be returned within 14 calendar days of receipt, as long as they arrive in perfect, unused condition. Personalised pieces, made-to-order commissions and illustration commissions cannot be returned, except in the case of a manufacturing fault or transport damage.

How do the pieces travel? +

With a lot of care. Figures and paintings are protected with padded materials, and whenever possible I reuse or reduce plastic. If your piece is fragile, it's clearly marked on the parcel for whoever receives it on your behalf.

How do I order an illustration commission? +

I normally don't accept illustration commissions, but if you like my style and would like me to make one for you, don't hesitate to get in touch through the contact form. I study the idea, create the sketch, and we close the commission.

How is a commission paid for? +

Once the quote is agreed, I'll send you the payment details through whichever channel we're using for the order (bank transfer or the platform we're working on). For larger pieces or commissions, I usually ask for a deposit when the order is confirmed and the rest before shipping.
