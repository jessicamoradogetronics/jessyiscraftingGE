Sir Henry of Skalitz

Hand-painted miniature of Sir Henry, with every detail of the armour worked with a fine brush. Layers of colour build up slowly to give depth to the metal and the fabrics, without losing the character of a figure made to be seen up close.

I chose the colours from the start of Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2, because I personally like seeing Henry in the colours of Leipa. I also customised the shield, flattening it so I could paint the Leipa emblem.

This photo session was special. I took this figure to the village of Skalitz in real life and photographed it on the only stone left from Skalitz Castle. In the end, Henry came home as Sir Henry.
