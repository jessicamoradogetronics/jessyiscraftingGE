Emote pack for Discord and Twitch

An emote pack drawn for a Twitch contest. Three expressions of the same character, each with its own gesture: writing with a pencil on a sheet of paper, raising a beer mug, and stirring a bubbling cauldron over the fire. Designed to read at a glance and work in chat at small sizes.
