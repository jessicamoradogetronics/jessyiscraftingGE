Special edition: Henry on horseback

A piece inspired by the Collector's Edition of Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2. I preferred to show Henry's face, without a helmet, conveying the strain of someone in battle. The weapon he carries is Sir Radzig's sword, which Henry will inherit in the future.

I personally like the colours of Leipa, so I chose yellows and blacks for the horse's harness, as well as the Leipa emblem on the shield Henry carries. I painted the horse in Pebbles' colours, a horse I am especially fond of.

To finish the figure, I added elements to imitate shrubs and undergrowth, giving the piece much more realism and depth.
